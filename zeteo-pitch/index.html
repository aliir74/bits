Method 04
Three modes of conversation

Qualitative research, at a scale you cannot reach by hand.

Categories emerge from what people said rather than being imposed in advance, and every claim stays attached to the sentence it came from.

  • Listening at scale. Reads what your audience already says, the mailbag, comments, social, transcripts, and organises it into themes, distinct opinions and verbatim quotes.
  • One at a time. An adaptive interview that asks follow-ups and probes reasons, on web, messaging or phone. Fifteen minutes each, thousands at once.
  • All together. A Polis-style conversation where the audience votes on one another's statements and adds their own, surfacing common ground no editor would find.
Listening at scale is built on Jigsaw's open-sourced tools, adapted by us 04 / 08
Scenario two · Retention and growth 05
The subscriber dialogue

One email, three audiences, answers in their own words.

Surveys flatten people into multiple choice; comment sections amplify the angriest. A dialogue answers what neither can: why people pay, why they leave, and what would bring them back.

  • One email and a landing page to current paid, churned and free-tier subscribers.
  • Anonymous participation available; any question skippable; no dark patterns.
  • All three interviews are already built and live, one per audience.
On who actually answers

Opinion groups and cross-group agreement do not depend on a representative sample the way a headcount does. We are not estimating what share of your readers believe something. We are finding which distinct positions exist, and which of them people across those positions will endorse.

The sampling question, answered up front.

The pilot · two to three weeks end to end 07

Your cost: three emails, one landing-page link, about four hours of one editor's time. We bring everything else.

Days 1–3
Design it with you

Working sessions with your editorial and growth teams. The interview guides exist; we tune goals, tone and boundaries together, and you approve every detail.

Weeks 1–2
The campaign runs

One email per segment, a landing page, interviews and the conversation on web and messaging, hosted on a Zeteo domain in Zeteo's branding.

Analysis runs continuously as answers come in. The explorable report is ready within hours of field close, not weeks.
